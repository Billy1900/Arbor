Metrics (Prometheus):
  GET /metrics on port {{ .Values.api.service.port }}

Register a runner node (run on the bare-metal KVM host):
  curl -X POST http://<api-address>/internal/runners/register \
    -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
    -d '{
      "runner_class": "fc-x86_64-v1",
      "address": "http://<runner-host>:9090",
      "arch": "x86_64",
      "firecracker_version": "1.9.0",
      "cpu_template": "T2",
      "capacity_slots": 10
    }'

Drain a runner before maintenance:
  curl -X PUT http://<api-address>/internal/runners/<runner-id>/drain
  # Wait for active VMs to finish, then deregister:
  curl -X DELETE http://<api-address>/internal/runners/<runner-id>
